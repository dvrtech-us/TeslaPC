Screen 📁 Files Settings
Stream resolution
Largest picture height sent to the car. Higher is sharper and uses more data. It never enlarges the picture and does not change the PC's own display. Applies immediately. A video that is already playing keeps its size until it is started again.
Fit screen The Fit screen button on the main screen sets both the PC's real display resolution and the stream resolution to suit the screen you tap it on. It picks the supported PC mode closest in shape that is at least as large as that screen, so the picture is sharp and black bars are small. TeslaPC does not restore the previous resolution afterwards.
Mobile Hotspot On publishes 100.64.0.1 for the car and forwards ports 8080 and 8443. Off stays off until you turn it on here. The 60 second watchdog will turn it back on only if it was not switched off here.
